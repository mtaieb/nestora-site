YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Nestora organizes your household’s grocery inventory and wardrobe. Data is stored on your device and in your household’s private or shared iCloud container. The developer does not operate a server that receives this data.

1. Information used by the app

Depending on the features you use, Nestora stores your household name, the member profiles you create, products and quantities, receipts and purchase details, as well as clothing, photos, characteristics, and storage locations. This information is used to display your inventory, track purchases, and organize your household.

2. Storage and iCloud synchronization

Information is saved locally. Synchronization and sharing use Apple’s iCloud and CloudKit services, associated with your Apple Account. Availability depends on your connection and iCloud settings. Apple’s processing is described in its privacy policy.

3. Sharing with household members

When you invite someone to join your household through iCloud, your household data becomes accessible to them through sharing. Choose participants carefully and manage invitations in Nestora’s settings. Creating a family profile, including for a child, does not automatically grant access to iCloud sharing.

4. Camera and receipt recognition

With your permission, the camera is used to scan receipts or photograph clothing. Photos you select are used for the corresponding features. Receipt recognition and analysis take place on the device using Apple technologies; no external image recognition service is used. Clothing photos may be synchronized with your household through iCloud.

5. Advertising and tracking

Nestora does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or third-party usage analytics. The developer does not sell your data and cannot access your household’s content through an external dashboard.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is retained so you can use your inventory and history. You can edit information and delete items where the app offers a deletion option. Deletions of shared data are synchronized through iCloud when a connection is available. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee deletion of data already stored in iCloud or copies held by other participants or in backups.

7. Feedback during the TestFlight beta

If you send feedback, a screenshot, or a crash report through TestFlight, Apple may share that information with the developer to improve the app. Avoid including unnecessary personal information in your feedback. See the TestFlight privacy information.

8. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add cookies, analytics tools, or data collection forms. GitHub may process technical information needed for hosting, as described in its privacy statement.

9. Contact and updates

For questions, contact the developer using the email address shown in TestFlight or the GitHub issue tracker. The issue tracker is public: do not post personal information or screenshots of your household there. This policy may change as Nestora’s features evolve; the update date appears at the top of this page.
