YOUR HOME, TOGETHER

Less searching.
More room for life.

Groceries, clothing, storage: Nestora brings the little details of your home together in one iPhone app to share with your family.

Beta available through TestFlight
01 / GROCERIES

Keep track of what you have.

Find your products, track remaining quantities, and record what you use so you always know what is left at home.

02 / RECEIPTS

Your purchases, without all the typing.

Scan your receipts, review the recognized items, and keep a history of your purchases and prices.

03 / WARDROBE

A place for every garment.

Organize clothing by family member, size, condition, and storage location. Add a photo to make each item easy to find.

A SHARED SPACE

One home. One family.
Everyone on the same page.

Create your household, customize your storage locations, and invite your family through iCloud to organize everyday life together.

Your data stays within your household.

App data is stored on your device and synchronized through iCloud. Receipt recognition takes place on your iPhone.
